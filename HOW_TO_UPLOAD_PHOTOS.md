# How to Upload Hike Photos in Walk Nepal Walk

Follow these simple steps to upload and share your Himalayan trek and hike photos with the community gallery:

## Step 1: Navigate to the Gallery
- Open the application and click on **Gallery** from the navigation bar (desktop header or mobile bottom tab navigation).

## Step 2: Open the Upload Modal
- Click on the **"Upload Photos"** or **"+"** button at the top of the Gallery screen.
- *(Note: If you are not signed in, you will be prompted to sign in securely with your account before uploading).*

## Step 3: Select Your Hike & Photos
1. **Choose Associated Trek**: Select the specific hike or trek from the dropdown list that corresponds to your photos.
2. **Add Caption (Optional)**: Type a short description, memory, or highlights about your hike.
3. **Select Image Files**: Click or drag and drop your photo files (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) into the upload box. You can select multiple high-resolution photos at once.

## Important Note: Daily Upload Limits
- **Regular Users**: Can upload up to **4 photos per day**.
- **Administrators**: Have unlimited daily photo uploads.

## Step 4: Complete Upload
- Click **Upload Photos**.
- A progress bar will show the upload status as your images are processed and securely stored to the community gallery.
- Once complete, your photos will immediately appear at the top of the Gallery feed for fellow hikers to view, like, and comment on!
